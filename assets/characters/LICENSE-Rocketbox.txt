The characters in this folder whose line in manifest.json says "license": "MIT" are
made from the avatars of the Microsoft Rocketbox library,
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox, which come under this licence.
The copies here are changed: thinned, retextured to one 1K set, their skeletons
renamed, and the zombies and clowns painted over. The blocky characters are
Kenney's (https://kenney.nl), CC0, for which no notice is needed.
